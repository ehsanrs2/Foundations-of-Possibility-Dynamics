# Working primary-source bibliography (reviewed 2026-10-26)

The list is a **starting bibliography, not a systematic review**. Publication dates are from publisher or institutional records, not inferred from DOI digits.

1. **Aubin, J.-P. (1991; 2009 reprint).** *Viability Theory*. Birkhäuser. [2009 reprint DOI: 10.1007/978-0-8176-4910-4](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-8176-4910-4). The 1991 original and 2009 reprint must not be conflated.
2. **Tria, F., Loreto, V., Servedio, V. D. P., & Strogatz, S. H. (2014).** The dynamics of correlated novelties. *Scientific Reports*, 4, 5890. [DOI: 10.1038/srep05890](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep05890). Novelty and adjacent-possible dynamics.
3. **Klyubin, A. S., Polani, D., & Nehaniv, C. L. (2005).** All Else Being Equal Be Empowered. In *Advances in Artificial Life*, pp. 744–753. [DOI: 10.1007/11553090_75](https://doi.org/10.1007/11553090_75). Empowerment as information-theoretic action-channel capacity.
4. **Di Bona, G., Bellina, A., De Marzo, G., et al. (2025).** The dynamics of higher-order novelties. *Nature Communications*, 16, 393. [DOI: 10.1038/s41467-024-55115-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-55115-y). Published 4 January 2025 despite the DOI containing 024.
5. **Loreto, V., Servedio, V. D. P., & Tria, F. (2026).** *The Science of the New*. Oxford University Press. [DOI: 10.1093/9780191834615.001.0001](https://doi.org/10.1093/9780191834615.001.0001). Online publication 26 February 2026; important prior work on quantitative novelty and innovation.
6. **Luo, C., Liu, Y., Li, P., & Wang, W. (2025).** Resilience of interdependent hypergraphs in failure–recovery spreading process. *Physica A*, 675, 130788. [DOI: 10.1016/j.physa.2025.130788](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physa.2025.130788). Related higher-order failure-recovery models.
